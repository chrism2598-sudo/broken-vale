Procedural (made in code, no external asset): the Hollow Beacon endgame — four bronze morning mirrors, the Unspent carving (canvas-drawn), light beams, the Updraft standing stones, wind column and motes, the sunrise sky shell (shader) and the parchment map (canvas-drawn from the terrain height field); the Vale Warden guardian (stone/copper/timber construct with articulated arms, glowing core and moss — no rigged model; stone and metal use the CC0 Mossy Rock and Rusty Metal 02 sets above), its strike-zone ring and dust shader; Wind Shrine orrery rings, wind ribbons, rune stones and canvas-drawn rune glyphs / tablet carving (stone uses the CC0 Mossy Rock set above); favicon; landmark detail (string courses, lit windows, braziers, banners, forge yard props, chimney smoke and waterfall mist sprites drawn on a canvas, lantern posts, base grime shading); the Hollow Beacon's violet/gold beam; landmark architecture (keep walls/towers/gatehouses, Sunspire lathe tower and sunwheel, Iron Dome, Veilfall bluff, waterfall and rotunda, Hollow Beacon pillars) built from primitives and textured with the CC0 sets above; seal plinths, crystals and light columns; grass blades, creek water shader tweaks, floating island geometry, waterfall, rift shard, the Wayfinder's lantern staff, sky rift, snow/haze terrain shading.
Impostor cards for distant trees are rendered at load time from the same CC0 tree models.
Each Poly Pizza model page above was checked on 2026-11-09 and shows "Public Domain (CC0)".
