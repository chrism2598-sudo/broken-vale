WHAT WAS WRONG (measured on M58)
- Wind-ups were telegraphed only on the foe. In 8 of 8 arenas, no touch button showed which control answers a wind-up.
- In the Sea Cave the threat pill said "Warden" and showed nothing for the Tide-Wights. The keep, coast and island could also show a stale Warden pill.
- No screen feedback for a blocked blow, or for an action that failed for lack of stamina.
- For about 4 s after a fight, the action button drew over the tip banner. On a 360x740 phone the tip covered up to 44% of the hero.
- Standing by a seal facing away from it, the camera clipped through the crystal (5 of 5 test angles).

WHAT CHANGED (existing systems only)
1. Wind-up cue, game-wide: when a foe in striking range winds up (husks, cultists, shades, sentinels, barrow foes and the King, Tide-Wights, the Warden strike, the Keeper), the Roll and Block buttons get an amber ring and the threat pill goes to alert. CSS only: no new meshes or draw calls.
2. The Sea Cave shows a proper Tide-Wight pill (name, HP, hearts). The pill is hidden in scenes it doesn't belong to.
3. Feedback: a short cool-grey edge flash on a block, and the stamina bar shakes when an action fails for lack of stamina. The M58 hurt and parry flashes are unchanged.
4. While the action button is showing, the tip banner sits above it. On short phones (800 px tall or less) the tip is smaller.
5. Seal camera: when a seal stands between the camera and the player, the camera tilts up smoothly until it sees over the crystal. Distance, yaw and movement are unchanged.
6. Coaching stays opening-only (the M58 rules, unchanged). No new or repeated tutorial prompts.
- Unchanged: graphics and detail, Lite adaptive resolution, Smooth 30, world, landmarks, NPC lines, story, enemy stats and timings, save format, M11 movement.
