THE BROKEN VALE - M60 BRIEF (progression and rewards)
Status: release candidate tagged locally as release-M60 (commit 1512ea0, bundle index-0kZfz36t.js). NOT deployed. Production is still M59. Rollback point: release-M54.

WHAT THE AUDIT FOUND (docs/m60-audit.md; computed from the shipping code, plus an emulated run)
- Levels did nothing. Level = XP/50 was display-only: no stat, heart or damage depended on it. A level-up was a " · Level N" suffix on a toast.
- So every chest, stash, lookout, secret and discovery (about 50 of roughly 60 reward sources pay XP only) had no mechanical value.
- Seals 2-4 paid +25 XP and a counter, no more than a stash. After seal 1 nothing said what was next.
- Weapon overlap:
  - The Knight's Longsword was strictly beaten by the Tide-blade and the Harpoon, and outclassed by Emberbrand.
  - The Spear did less damage per second than the starting sword.
  - The Halberd said "longest reach" but the Harpoon reached further.
  - The weapon picker hid speed, stagger and stamina cost.
- The end screen showed "xp/580", a total last updated in M16. Thorough players see something like "1900/580".
- Main-path levels: Level 3 after four seals, Level 7 at the end of Chapter I, Level 12 at the end. A thorough player reaches Level 35-45.

WHAT CHANGED (existing systems only; no new currency, crafting, skill tree, content or save field)
1. Levels give power: blows +4% per level, up to Level 13 (+48%). It adds to whetstones (+15% each). It is derived from XP, so old saves get it automatically. The opening fight is unchanged (Level 1 = x1.00).
2. Level-up banner with a chime, from any XP source, e.g. "Level 2 - blows +4% - next at 100 XP". It is never shown on load and doesn't overlap the controls, the tip or the toast.
3. Progress you can read:
   - HUD: "Lv 5 - 230/250 XP".
   - Journal header: the bonus and the next level.
   - Weapon picker: total blow bonus (level + whetstones), plus speed, reach, stagger and heavy stamina cost per weapon.
4. Seals: +50 XP each (a level). From seal 2 on, the toast says "2/4 - 2 seals left - clues in your Journal". The opening coach tip covers seal 1.
5. Weapon roles (stat tuning only):
   - Spear: a bit more damage and stagger.
   - Knight: faster, with the cheapest heavy.
   - Ember: still the hardest hitter, but slower and stamina-hungry.
   - Halberd: truly the longest reach, every hit staggers.
   - Harpoon: slightly shorter reach, still quick with a Keeper bonus.
   - Rusty Sword, Maul and Tide-blade unchanged.
6. The end screen shows "Level N - X XP".
- Unchanged: story, dialogue, world, art, enemies, controls, opening coach rules, movement, graphics, adaptive resolution, Smooth 30, save format.

TEST-HARNESS CRASH INVESTIGATION
- In the first chained run, qa-save and rollback stopped with environment errors, not test failures:
  - qa-save: 126 checks passed and none failed, then the browser frame detached.
  - rollback: it timed out loading the first page (240 s), before any check.
- At the time the shared box had only 2.2-3.4 GB free. My visible processes totaled about 5 GB of the 12+ GB in use, so most of the pressure came from outside them.
- Both local servers stayed healthy (HTTP 200, correct bundles). The same bundle booted fine through the 13 suites, qa-run and the other gates.
- Rerun one at a time on the same commit, with the harnesses unchanged: qa-save 145/145 (started at 3.8 GB free, lowest 2.1 GB); rollback 20/20 (started at 7.7 GB, lowest 3.9 GB).
- Conclusion: an environment problem (low memory), not a game or save defect.
