WHAT CHANGED
1. Seal guards. After the first seal, each unclaimed seal gets a small
   group of existing foes on its approach, outside the campfire ring.
   The seal says "Guarded" until they fall.
   Seal 2: Sentinel + Husk. Seal 3: + Sentinel. Seal 4: + Gloam Brute.
2. Difficulty curve. Roaming foes get tougher as seals are claimed:
   HP +10 percent, wind-up 3 percent faster, speed +4 percent per seal,
   one more step in Chapter II, max 5 steps. The opening is unchanged.
   Damage is unchanged (whole hearts).
3. Coach tips spread out. The first fight teaches Attack and Roll.
   Block, parry and stamina tips wait for the first guard fight.
Kept: Wind Shrine Journal label. Not changed: Wayfinder dialogue and
the HUD "(optional)" shrine label.
