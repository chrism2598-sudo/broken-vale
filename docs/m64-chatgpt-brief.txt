WHAT CHANGED (before -> after)
1. Combat core. Stun-lock removed: foes break out ("poised") after 1.5 s
   of stagger (longest lock ~19.7 s -> ~1.8 s, computed). At most 2 foes
   wind up at once, 0.45 s apart. Foe tactics: Shade flanks, Brute rushes,
   Cultist steps back, Husk lurches, Sentinel guards its front (spears
   pierce). Riposte after a parry; rolling through a blow refunds stamina.
   Hearts come back one at a time when calm.
2. Feedback. Swing arc and sparks in the weapon's colour, a struck sound
   per weapon class, foes twist away from blows, ash on death, a wind-up
   voice per foe type, boss stingers. All procedural.
3. Kindling (signature mechanic). Parries and roll-throughs earn sparks;
   spend one on the attuned seal's power: Temper, Daybreak, Glare,
   Undertow. A kindled seal breaks the Vale Warden's slam.
4. Bosses. Title / phase / victory cards. Warden phase 2 follow-up slam;
   Ossric grave-sweep with a 1.3 s tell.
5. World. Three new ruins (Hearth Ruin, Reed Shrine, Shepherd's Rise):
   mend with a spark -> relic + lore page, on the map. Region banners and
   a fog tint per place. Rewards are relics and lore, not XP.
6. Progression. Perk choice at Lv 3/6/9/12 (8 perks). One relic slot,
   three relics. A group cleared on an earlier day pays half XP.
7. Story/UI. Skippable 3-line opening for new games; each seal has its
   own claim beat; fight music in overworld fights; the ending names how
   you played (e.g. "Turner of Blows") with your own counts.
8. Overlay fix (found in screenshot review): seal card no longer stacks
   on the claim toast + level banner; region banner off the quest pill.
9. Bug fix found by the emulated playthrough: after beating the Vale
   Warden, the game still thought it was awake for the rest of the
   session (since M63: campfire rest refused until reload; in M64 also
   no heart regen and no perk card). Fixed; full gates rerun.
