THE BROKEN VALE - NOTES FOR WATCHING SOMEONE PLAY

Before
- Use their phone, a fresh private/incognito tab, the live link.
- Say only: "It's an adventure game. Play however you like, stop whenever
  you want. I can't help, I'm just watching."

While they play
- Do not help, hint, point or answer questions. If asked, say
  "Do whatever you think" and write the question down.
- Stay quiet, don't react. Sit beside or behind them.
- Note the clock time when they start.
- Write down, with rough times:
  - where they pause, wander or look lost (and for how long)
  - what they tap, especially wrong buttons or repeated taps
  - whether they open the Menu or Journal on their own
  - whether they use Roll or Block, or only Attack
  - when they claim the first seal, and what they do right after
  - any lag, stutter, freezes or things covered on screen
  - anything they say out loud (word for word if you can)
  - when they stop, and what they were doing at that moment

After
- Ask "Why did you stop?" before anything else.
- Then send or read them the questionnaire.
- In the log, put what you SAW under OBSERVED and what they SAID
  under OPINION. Keep them separate.
